## 3. Experimental procedure

The existing Swift adapter reports production baseline, navigation-filtered, guarded, recent and equal-blend predictions. The present trials use `prediction = guarded_weight × guarded + (1 − guarded_weight) × recent`. The guarded and recent candidates are test-only. All predictions were obtained from saved historical Swift attachments; this run did not recompute them. The earlier batch manifests record different generator hashes; its saved candidate receipt is included as historical data, not asserted to have been produced from the current generator or adapter.

The 100 weights are exactly `i / 99` for integer i from 0 through 99. Development selection minimizes mean absolute percentage error across 250 development cases; ties choose the lowest index. The 72 comparison cases are separate saved cases but have previously been inspected: they are **not blind** and their scenario designs are not independent. The input order was shuffled with seed 947061. All trials use the same cases and fixed predictions; they are 100 weight comparisons, not 100 fresh simulations or independent experiments.

Absolute percentage error is `100 × |prediction − synthetic target| / synthetic target`. A spike is an absolute error greater than both 300 seconds and 75% of the target; this is a chosen investigation trigger, not a validated acceptance criterion. The 90th percentile uses the sorted value at Python index `round((n − 1) × 0.9)`; the median uses Python’s standard median. Precision of a weight is not precision of the estimate.

## 10. Interpretation and remaining risks

1. Synthetic future outcomes and book response effects were invented by generators. A small error means agreement with these assumptions, not demonstrated accuracy for real readers.
2. The reader adapter injects already-closed page records. Real sessions can have an open current page; PDF polling, EPUB mapping, background closure, and SQLite summary reload were not exercised. The previous audit identified these as potential differences; the present run does not resolve them.
3. The histories used for selection and comparison were generated and inspected in earlier work. The apparent comparison set is neither fresh nor independently blinded, and repeated fitting may overfit it.
4. The first saved adversarial batch used a different recorded generator digest from the later batches. Do not assume identical generator versions or data-generating distributions. Compare manifests and saved source receipts before treating differences as causal.
5. A grid and an exact search across a single two-candidate blend do not explore every formula or establish the “best possible” weights. More decimal places do not fix missing information or biased synthetic targets.
6. **SKIPPED: individual grading of every case and round**, **SKIPPED: fresh all-profile predictions**, and **SKIPPED: real-reader validation**. A reviewer and a resolved file-access issue are needed before making those claims.
7. No production estimator source, release, commit or push was changed by this experiment. The working tree already contained unrelated and earlier-session modifications; the report does not claim a clean checkout. The test host launched the app; no inspection was made of the user's persisted library.
